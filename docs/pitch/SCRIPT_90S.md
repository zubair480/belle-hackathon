# Ninety-second demo script

Focus: manual issue creation, verified fix reuse, one evidence-linked insight. Say "sample data" first if the run is in mock mode.

**0:00 – 0:15**
"An EV fails final inspection: the charge-port connector is misaligned. The connector is a purchased part; the bracket under it is made in-house. RecallRadius traces both and reuses a verified fix."

**0:15 – 0:40 · Vehicles**
Tap the charge port on the sketch. "Zoomed in: supplier batch and receipt for the connector, manufacturing lot, work order and team for the bracket. The team is shown as producer, not cause." Click Report issue on this part, save. "A real saved record, reported by Final Inspection, no cause confirmed yet, no import or AI needed."

**0:40 – 1:10 · Resolution**
"The graph finds the prior bracket fix verified on DEMO-EV-002: same defect code, same part, with its limits." Click Reuse. "It becomes a proposal labelled from a verified prior resolution. Close is disabled until this vehicle passes its own verification." Record a pass, close. "Closed with the verified fix; the history is kept, and the next issue can retrieve it."

**1:10 – 1:30 · Insights**
"Reported, assigned and confirmed-cause counts are separate. Final Inspection reported three issues and caused none. The connector supplier is linked to two issues, confirmed on one, with a rate only because a complete inspection cohort exists. Click any count to see the issues and evidence. Next: a one-site pilot measuring investigation time and fix reuse."
